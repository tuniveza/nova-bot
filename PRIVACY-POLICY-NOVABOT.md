NovaBot, our website chat assistant

Our website has a chat assistant called NovaBot that answers questions about the studio, our services and bookings. NovaBot is an AI assistant, not a person.

What we collect

- The messages you type or speak in the chat, and which page of our website you were on.
- If you ask NovaBot to send an enquiry to our team: your name, email address, phone number (if you give it) and the details of your enquiry.
- A scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address, used only to stop spam. We don't store your IP address itself.

Please don't share sensitive personal information (such as health or payment details) in the chat.

How we use it

- To answer your questions and pass your enquiry to our team, so we can reply to you by email.
- To review conversations and improve NovaBot's answers.

We rely on our legitimate interest in answering questions about our services and improving them, and, for enquiries, on taking steps at your request before you book with us.

Who processes it for us

- Anthropic, whose AI model (Claude) writes NovaBot's replies. Your messages are sent to Anthropic to generate each reply.
- Cloudflare, which runs NovaBot and stores the conversations and enquiries. If you use the microphone or voice replies, Cloudflare's AI services turn your speech into text and NovaBot's replies into speech. Recordings aren't stored.
- Email copies: Resend, which emails your enquiry to our team.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK, with safeguards in place as required by UK data protection law.

How long we keep it

- Chat conversations are deleted automatically after 90 days.
- Enquiries are kept for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and any booking that follows, then deleted.

Stored on your device

NovaBot uses your browser's storage to keep the conversation going as you move between pages (cleared when you close the tab) and to remember whether you turned voice replies on.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing info@novacane.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
